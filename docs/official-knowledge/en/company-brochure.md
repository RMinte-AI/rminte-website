Introduction
In 2026, rapid model releases brought stronger
capabilities, lower costs and a shift from model
competition to deployment.
Models are now accessible; getting them into
enterprise operations is the challenge. Cloud AI
needs constant connectivity, while central servers
need space, power and cooling. Remote or harsh
sites need AI that runs locally, moves with the task
and controls operating cost.
Our answer: industry applications + open models +
edge inference + cloud collaboration.
Portable AI combines offline operation with ultra-
low power, bringing data-center-class inference to
the field.
