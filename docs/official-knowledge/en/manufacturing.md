## Materials, finish, and fit. A standard for every detail.

![Aluminium chassis craftsmanship detail](https://rminte.com/assets/gallery/DSC00150.jpg)

6063

### Aluminium chassis

The chassis is made from 6063 aluminium alloy. Its solid feel in your hand begins with the material itself.

![Fine bead-blasted finish detail](https://rminte.com/assets/images/craft-surface-detail.webp)

260 mesh

### Fine sandblasted finish

Sandblasting gives the metal a fine texture you can see and feel.

![Detail of the rounded glass corners and aluminium chassis assembly](https://rminte.com/assets/images/craft-panel-fit.webp)

R0.2 mm

### Rounded glass edges

Glass edges with an R0.2 mm radius form a precision fit with the aluminium chassis. Where the two materials meet, the care in assembly comes into view.

![Manufacturing tolerance detail](https://rminte.com/assets/images/craft-card-slot-detail.webp)

＜0.03 mm

### Manufacturing tolerance

Manufacturing tolerances stay below 0.03 mm. That precision shapes the seams and edges, rewarding a closer look at every joint.
