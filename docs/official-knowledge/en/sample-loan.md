# Applying for a sample loan

Open “Contact” on the website and choose the sample-loan application. No case-system account is required. Enter borrower, contact and shipping details, model and requested days. Read the agreement and key notices, review the confirmation, then confirm agreement and submit. Submission does not mean approval; staff approve the equipment and actual loan period.

## Confirm before submitting

Read the full loan agreement (Chinese)

Submitting means agreeing to the RM-01 Product Loan Agreement and the following key points

- **Deposit and remittance** The deposit is RMB 30,000 or the USD equivalent at the fixed quote in the confirmation email. Remit within 24 hours of delivery of that email. Shipment requires full receipt and verified identity and authority.
- **Return transport and insurance** **Outbound: freight collect, with full-value insurance arranged by the manufacturer. Return: pay freight and insurance yourself, insuring the full machine value stated in the agreement. The applicant bears freight and insurance costs in both directions.**
- **Return and extension** The loan period includes transport both ways. The machine and all accessories must reach the manufacturer before the deadline. Apply for an extension at least seven calendar days in advance. Terms of seven days or less cannot be extended. Without approval, the original deadline remains.
- **Overdue fees and compensation** The administrator sets the complete machine value on approval. Overdue return and overdue compensation payment are each charged at 1% of that value per day; the same period is not charged twice. Damage, missing items or loss attributable to the borrower are settled from the deposit first; any shortfall is payable separately.
- **Power-off handling** The three specified ports beneath the magnetic cover may be connected or disconnected only with the device powered off. The storage card is recorded by its visible serial number. Damage from hot-plugging requires payment for a new card of the same model; if unavailable, an internationally known brand with equivalent technical specifications is used as stated in the agreement.
- **Restore account information** **If you change the demo unit’s default account information or passwords, restore the initial state recorded at delivery before return, so the original account and password can log in normally.**
- **Email acknowledgment and refund** Reply to acknowledge the agreement within 48 hours after its email is delivered. No reply is treated as acknowledgment under the agreed terms; a known bounce does not start the clock. Refunds follow normal return within five working days, in the original currency: CNY to CNY and USD to USD. Outstanding amounts are handled under the agreement.

Agree and continue

We retain the application, terms version, key notices and confirmation for loan administration and traceability. See the full agreement for data handling, retention and rights.
